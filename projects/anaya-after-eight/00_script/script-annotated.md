this file holds the timings, visual direction and on-screen TEXT.

- VO: "Clinic owners, if you're running Meta ads, open your WhatsApp Business tonight."
- TEXT: CLINIC OWNERS: who replies after 8pm?

## [3–10s] PROBLEM
- Visual: screen recording — WhatsApp Business inbox, clinic's view, scrolling past unread
  chats timestamped 8:12 PM, 9:04 PM, 10:37 PM. Names blurred.
- VO: "Scroll to everything that came in after eight. Every unanswered chat is a lead you already paid for."

## [17–21s] REFRAME

- VO: "It's not your team. The desk closes at eight. The enquiries don't."

  then a day-5 follow-up bubble.

- VO: "Same number. Same team. Same system."
