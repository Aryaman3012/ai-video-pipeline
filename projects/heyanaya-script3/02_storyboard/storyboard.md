# HeyAnaya — Script 3 · Storyboard

Podcast explainer for UAE clinic-chain owners, ~45 s + 3.2 s end card, 16:9 master + 9:16.
Seven clips, one per line, host centred at the studio mic (`podcast-host`, Veo 3.1 Fast, first
frame `01_vision/refs/host_podcast.jpg`). The clips already exist in `04_clips/`; the prompts in
`03_prompts/` are what they were generated from.

**Look:** brand navy `#0E1B24` grounds, teal `#14B8A6` for HeyAnaya / solved states, amber for
"unanswered", generic green chat UI (no WhatsApp branding). Problem beats graded cool, solution
and close warm. Arabic patient messages throughout (Gulf dialect) with a small grey English line
beneath, so the "preferred language" beat pays off a thread started in the hook.

**Story through the graphics:** one phone with a few unread chats → the same inbox at every
branch → one of those chats is a patient you paid for → a whole month of missed slots across the
chain → HeyAnaya answers *that same patient*, in Arabic, and fills *that same empty slot* → more
leads just leak → the same leads, sealed, become bookings.

**Layout rhythm:** full→split · pip · full→split · full→pip · full→split · full→pip · split → end card.

Timings below are approximate (from the prompt timing); the edit resolves every cue on the spoken word.

## Notes and justifications

- **Clip 05 is 22 words (~8.1 s at 2.7 w/s) against a 7.4 s budget.** The validator warns
  "split the line" and the prompt lint warns it averages 3.0 words/s. It is kept as one clip
  because it already exists (log.md: seven clips, one per line) and the words may not be changed.
  At ingest, check the last word ("appointments") isn't clipped and the read doesn't sound rushed;
  if it is, regenerate it as two clips split after "language,".
- Every clip's prompt opens with 0.4 s `[SILENCE]` and ends on a `[PAUSE]` tail.
- Branch names, the patient name, AED 95 per lead, 960 missed opportunities and "Replied in 3 s"
  are invented placeholders and are marked Illustrative.
